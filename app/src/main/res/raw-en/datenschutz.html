What data does the app process?
The app reads the machine-readable zone (MRZ) of ID cards, passports and visas with the camera or accepts it by manual entry. It contains personal data such as name, date of birth, document number, nationality and expiry date.

Where is the data processed?
Exclusively on the device. Text recognition (Google ML Kit) runs locally with a model included in the app. The app has no internet permission and therefore cannot transmit any data – neither to the publisher of the app nor to Google or any other third party.

Usage statistics of Google ML Kit
Google designed the text recognition library to send anonymous technical usage statistics (e.g. number and duration of recognitions, device model) – no images and no recognised text. In this app the transmission path has been removed, and without internet permission nothing can be sent anyway. Queued statistics are discarded.

Operating system diagnostics
Android's system diagnostics (e.g. crash reports) depend on the device settings and are not controlled by the app. They can be switched off in the Android settings under Google or Privacy.

Is data stored?
No. Camera images and read data are kept only in memory while the result page is displayed. There is no database, no history, no logs and no backup (Android backup is disabled). When the app is closed, the data is gone. When the screen is locked or the app goes to the background, it automatically discards all displayed data and returns to the empty camera view. The only things stored are certificate lists that you import yourself (public root certificates of the issuing states, no personal data). They can be deleted in the app via “Remove all”. Screenshots and screen recordings are blocked in the app, and no preview appears in the list of recently used apps.

Chip check via NFC (optional)
On request, the app uses the card access number (CAN) or the data of the printed MRZ to read the digital MRZ from the document's chip and verifies its signature. Facial image and fingerprints are not read. This data, too, stays in memory only and is neither stored nor transmitted.

Permissions
Camera – solely for reading the MRZ. Without camera permission, manual entry is still possible. NFC – solely for the optional chip check; range a few centimetres, no internet connection.

No analytics, no advertising
The app contains no tracking, no analytics tools and no advertising.

Your rights
As the publisher receives no data, he holds no data about you. You nevertheless have the rights under Art. 15–21 GDPR and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

Note on use
